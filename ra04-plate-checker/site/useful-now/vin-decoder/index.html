What this tool does — and does not do

It decodes technical information reported by vehicle manufacturers to NHTSA. It does not provide ownership history, accident history, mileage history, liens or a guarantee about the vehicle’s condition.

Your VIN is sent directly from your browser to the official NHTSA vPIC API for decoding. This page does not have its own backend that stores the VIN.
